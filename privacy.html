Privacy Policy – Clipping uploader

Last updated: November 2026

Clipping uploader is a personal application used only by the owner of the connected YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The app requests only the Google permission youtube.upload, used exclusively to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

Storage

The authorization token is stored privately by the owner (on their own computer and in a private, encrypted secret store) and is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
